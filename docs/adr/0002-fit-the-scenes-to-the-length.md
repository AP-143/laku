# Fit the scenes to the length, not the length to the scenes

The Seller picks a Length of 10, 20 or 30 seconds, and LAKU fits the Storyboard to it. One Clip is made per Frame and a Clip runs 4 to 8 seconds, so 20 and 30 seconds use all four Frames (with longer Clips), while 10 seconds only has room for two: it keeps the **opening and the closing Scene** (the hook and the call to action) and leaves the middle out. Which Frames are used depends only on how many Clips there are, so a video remade after a failed Clip picks the same ones again.

## Considered options

- **Let the Seller type any length.** Rejected: an odd length ends with a cut-off scene, the price is hard to predict, and four fixed prices (about US$1, US$2 and US$3) are easy to understand and to guard.
- **Always use the first N Frames.** What the first version did. Rejected: a 10 second video then ended before the call to action, so the ad never asked anyone to buy.
- **Ask a model which Scenes to keep.** Rejected: one more paid call, and a different answer on each try, which breaks remaking a failed video the same way.

## Consequences

- A 10 second video does not show the two middle Scenes. A Seller who wants every Scene picks 20 or 30 seconds.
- The Clip length limits (4 to 8 seconds) come from the video model; 8 second Clips are still untried on the real model.
